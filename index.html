how to read this scanner ▾
signal badges
BULL 1H ↗
Price made 3 lower lows while RSI made 3 higher lows. Momentum building under falling price — potential reversal up. The TF label (4H / 1H / 30M) shows where it was detected. Click the badge to open TradingView.
BULL ⚡ 4H ↗
Same setup with above-average volume on Drive 3 (1.5× the 20-candle average). Real buying pressure behind the move — stronger signal.
BEAR 4H ↗
Price made 3 higher highs while RSI made 3 lower highs. Momentum fading at higher prices — potential reversal down.
BULL 1H ↗ 3×
The scan count (3×) shows how many consecutive scans this signal appeared. A signal at 3× has persisted for 90 minutes on auto-scan — significantly more reliable than a first-time detection.
bb squeeze
◈ SQZ 4H
Bollinger Bands at their tightest point in the last 125 candles. Volatility compressed — a large move is loading. Only fires at 70%+ compression strength.
◈ SQZ 1H ◎
Squeeze at a key S/R level simultaneously. Compression at a structurally significant price zone — highest probability squeeze setup. The ◎ shows "support" or "resistance".
BW 1.8 · 74%
BW = BandWidth value — how wide the bands are. Lower = tighter. 74% = compression strength relative to the last 125 bars. Higher % = stronger squeeze about to break.
confidence score
Scored 0–99% from three components:

RSI divergence — how far RSI moved against price.

Price divergence — how much price moved across the three drives.

Time symmetry — whether the drives are evenly spaced.
≥75% = gold left border on the row. When FVG confluence is detected on the same TF, confidence gets a bonus boost of up to +15%.
82% NEW
NEW = detected in the current scan. Clears on the next scan run.
3 scans
Signal has appeared in 3 consecutive scans. Persistent signals carry more conviction than a single appearance.

⬦ BULL FVG 4H [B]

Requires 2+ full VAH-to-VAL swings.
